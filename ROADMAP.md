# TaxNest release plan

## Batch 1 (included)
Native, offline-first Android foundation; receipt image vault with OCR and manual review; personal ledger; subscription entries; mileage; overtime estimate; tax break prompts; credit score snapshot; CSV export; Mi Transfer/Mi Tidy-inspired UI.

## Batch 2 (next)
- Receipt duplicate detection (image hash + purchase matching), multi-photo purchase records.
- Search/filter by date, amount, category, store, work/personal/review, and full OCR text.
- Add export ZIP with all original receipt photos plus CSV, plus user-controlled encrypted backup.
- Detailed vehicle records: service dates, maintenance, fuel receipts, odometer start/end, truck allowance reimbursements, expenses vs employer-paid items.
- Manual budget goals, subscriptions calendar, ZEUS Truck Fund goal, debt repayment scenario calculator.
- Receipt-to-transaction matching and editable item lines.

## Batch 3 (requires separate access/approval)
- Google OAuth for optional Gmail receipts, with permission minimization, consent, revocation and restricted-scope verification as required.
- Optional supported bank connections or statement-file imports (no credential scraping).
- Paycheck PDF import; W-2 box 12 TT overtime reconciliation; tax rule packs by year and state.
- BoreLab Field mileage import and privacy-safe exports.
- Pixel polish to match approved concept on real OnePlus and tablet screens.
